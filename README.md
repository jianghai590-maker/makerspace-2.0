# Makerspace 2.0 — Collaborative Online Creative Hub

Discovery and requirements work for a collaborative online creative platform: understanding who the stakeholders are, what they need, and turning those needs into a buildable, governable product plan.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Define the vision, requirements, and governance for a "Makerspace 2.0" — an online hub where creators can collaborate, share work, and build together.

## 👤 My Role

Led **discovery and requirements synthesis**: facilitated ideation and prioritization sessions, mapped stakeholders, and translated qualitative findings into concrete delivery artifacts.

## 🔬 Methods & Activities

- **Stakeholder mapping** — identified key stakeholder groups, their interests, and influence
- **Risk register** — surfaced and tracked project risks with mitigation strategies
- **User-journey sketches** — mapped end-to-end journeys for core creator workflows
- **Backlog items & acceptance criteria** — broke the vision into prioritized, testable work items
- **Ideation facilitation** — ran structured brainstorming and prioritization with the team

## 📦 Key Deliverables

- Stakeholder maps
- Risk register
- User-journey sketches
- Prioritized product backlog with acceptance criteria
- Low-fidelity prototype
- Governance recommendations for ongoing platform management

## 💡 Outcome

Turned open-ended qualitative findings into a **low-fidelity prototype** backed by a clear requirements package — giving the team a shared, testable vision of what to build and how to run it.
